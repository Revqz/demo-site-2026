About us

SIA “Aizkraukles Saldumi” is a confectionery manufacturer in Aizkraukle since 2002. We make zephyr, marmalade and glazes and supply them to retailers in the Baltics, Europe and the USA.

A trusted manufacturer of confectionery ingredients and fillings

SIA “Aizkraukles Saldumi” is one of the leading manufacturers of confectionery ingredients, supplying food companies with top-quality glazes and fillings.

At our modern plant in Aizkraukle we combine industry experience with a flexible approach to each customer’s needs. With stable production volumes and guaranteed traceability of raw materials, we have become a reliable long-term partner on the local market and export to the Baltics, Western Europe and the USA.

Why manufacturers choose us

Proven, natural ingredients

We do not use cheap substitutes — our zephyr and fillings are made only from high-quality raw materials. If a recipe calls for condensed milk, we use real milk, and we add natural apple purée and real jams.

Flexibility and innovation (Private Label)

We fully adapt production to your unique needs. We are open to innovation: for example, at the request of foreign partners we developed and now produce a special sugar-free chocolate.

Stability and on-time delivery

In large-scale production, rhythm is everything. We understand this, so we guarantee precise delivery dates and consistently high quality in every batch, without exception.

Our growth milestones

To keep quality consistent as volumes grow, we regularly invest in our development.

  1. 2002

    We start producing high-quality cocoa glazes and fillings for food companies in the Baltics and Europe, using carefully selected raw materials.

  2. 2007

    We install two modern zephyr production lines, which quickly reach high capacity thanks to strong demand and open up wide export opportunities.

  3. 2010

    We open a new line for natural marmalade cast in silicone moulds. Using top-quality pectin, production capacity reaches 60 tonnes per month.

  4. 2016

    We start producing pastila and the classic “Bird’s milk” dessert. The traditional recipe uses real butter and condensed milk.

  5. Development today

    The aim of project No. 25-00-C0LA0402-000046 is to modernise zephyr production by introducing an innovative, fully automated production line. As a result, production capacity and efficiency will increase, product quality and hygiene will improve, the range will expand and the company’s competitiveness on export markets will be strengthened. The project runs until 31 May 2027 and is supported by the Ministry of Agriculture and the Rural Support Service of Latvia.

What confectionery fillings and glazes do you produce?

We produce confectionery glazes based on cocoa powder, white and flavoured glazes, glazes for ice cream, as well as confectionery fillings and pastes. Sugar-free options with maltitol or isomalt are also available.

Do you offer private label production?

Yes, private label production is one of our core areas. We offer full-cycle cooperation — from the idea to the finished product — helping food companies expand their range with high-quality products under their own brand.

Can you develop a unique glaze or filling recipe?

Yes. We adapt glazes and fillings to each customer individually — by cocoa powder and sugar content, organoleptic properties, colour and taste.

What raw materials do you use?

We use only high-quality raw materials: cocoa butter substitutes from Indonesia, Malaysia and Europe, natural ingredients and premium pectin, real butter and condensed milk.

What are the delivery options and which regions do you export to?

We supply customers in Latvia, Lithuania and Estonia, and export to other European countries and the USA. Delivery terms are agreed for each order.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for B2B wholesale?

The minimum quantity depends on the product and packaging type. Contact us and we will prepare an offer for your needs.

Where can I buy “Torņu ielas saldumi” products?

Our products are available through partners and distributors in the Baltics. To find the nearest point of sale, write to us at info@tornu-saldumi.lv.
